PEDALSYNC

Free your Echelon from the subscription
PedalSync connects directly to your Echelon equipment over Bluetooth and gives you real-time telemetry — no subscription, no app install, no account. Just open this page in Chrome or Edge, tap connect, and ride.

HAVING TROUBLE?
If your equipment has a built-in screen, close the Echelon app on it first — the tablet holds the Bluetooth connection and only one device can use it at a time.
Most connection problems are one of these:
  • The equipment screen is asleep — pedal or press a button to wake it
  • Another app or the Echelon tablet is holding the connection — close it and turn its Bluetooth off
  • Bluetooth is off on this device
Fixed one of those? Tap CONNECT again. Still stuck? Email us — the message is pre-filled with what we need.
