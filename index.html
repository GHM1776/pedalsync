PEDALSYNC

Free your Echelon from the subscription
PedalSync connects directly to your Echelon equipment over Bluetooth and gives you real-time telemetry — no subscription, no app install, no account. Just open this page on your phone, laptop, or tablet, tap connect, and ride.
WHAT YOU GET
  • ▸ Real-time cadence, resistance & power
  • ▸ AI-powered workout coach
  • ▸ Works on locked firmware (v3.12+)
  • ▸ Export workouts to Strava & Garmin
  • ▸ No app install — runs in Chrome
